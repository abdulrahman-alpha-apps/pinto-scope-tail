# Feature Summary — Cost Center / Project-Level P&L Tracking

## Feature Name
Cost Center — Project-Level Profitability Tracking

## Description
This feature enables Pinto users to tag individual expenses and invoices to named projects or jobs, and then view a profitability breakdown per project on demand. When recording an expense or creating an invoice via WhatsApp, the user is optionally asked which project the transaction belongs to. Pinto aggregates all tagged revenue and costs per project and surfaces a project-level P&L report through the Reporting Agent on WhatsApp and on the Web App dashboard.

Untagged transactions are grouped under a "General" project by default. Projects are created and managed through WhatsApp conversation. The number of projects per organisation is configurable.

## User Segment
Primary beneficiaries (in priority order):

| Segment | Why This Matters |
|---|---|
| Digital Agencies | Track profitability per client campaign or retainer |
| Construction Subcontractors | Track costs and billing per job site or contract |
| Professional Services (Law, Consulting, Engineering) | Separate billable vs. overhead costs per client engagement |
| Auto Services / Workshops | Track costs and revenue per vehicle job |
| Transport & Delivery | Track costs per route, fleet vehicle, or delivery contract |

## Problem Solved
Business owners across project-based industries have no way to know whether individual projects or clients are profitable. All revenue and costs are currently tracked at the organisation level only. A construction firm may be profitable overall but losing money on a specific job. A digital agency may have one client generating all their margin while others are running at a loss. Without project-level visibility, these owners cannot price accurately, stop unprofitable work, or make informed business decisions.

## Why It Matters to Pinto
This is a **P0 strategic feature** in the Pinto backlog. It unlocks five high-priority segments simultaneously — Digital Agencies, Construction, Professional Services, Auto Services, and Transport — all of which list the absence of project-level tracking as a primary gap preventing full adoption of Pinto.
